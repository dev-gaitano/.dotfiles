The terms relate to the major transition in **Hyprland (v0.55+)** moving away from the legacy `hyprlang` format (`hyprland.conf`) to native **Lua configurations** (`hyprland.lua`)[1][2]. 

Here is how `killactive` maps and translates under this shift:

### 1. The Migration Context (`hyprlang2lua`)
[`hyprlang2lua`](https://github.com/EIonTusk/hyprlang2lua) is a utility tool designed to help users convert their old `hyprland.conf` files into the new Hyprland Lua format[1][3]. 

### 2. Translating `killactive` to Lua
In legacy configurations, closing the active window via dispatcher looked like this:
```ini
bind = $mainMod, Q, killactive,
```

In the modern Hyprland Lua API format, legacy action names and dispatchers have been modernized or structured into helper functions (such as `hl.dsp.window.close()` or mapped dispatcher parameters)[1][4]:
* **Legacy name:** `killactive` (which gracefully closes a window despite its name)[1]
* **New Lua equivalent:** Maps to window closing routines like `.close()`[1] or `hl.dsp.window.close()` depending on how your keybind helper is structured[4]. 

If you are using `hyprlang2lua` to automate the porting of your dotfiles, it automatically translates legacy vocabularies (like changing `killactive` over to the updated string/dispatcher format required by Hyprland 0.55+)[1].
